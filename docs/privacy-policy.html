Privacy Policy

App: Aransaga
Developer: Andreas Schulz
Last updated: September 4, 2026

Summary

Aransaga does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. All game progress is stored on your device and, if you use iCloud, in your personal iCloud account so it can sync between your devices. The developer has no access to it. Optional in-app purchases and optional Game Center achievements are processed entirely by Apple.

Data We Store

  • Story progress (current chapter, choices/flags, collected items, sidequest status, chapter chronicle) — stored locally via UserDefaults and synced through iCloud (see below).
  • Settings (music volume) — stored locally via UserDefaults. Never leaves your device.

iCloud Sync

If your device is signed in to iCloud, Aransaga stores a copy of your game progress in your personal iCloud account (Apple's iCloud key-value storage) so that it syncs between your own devices. This data stays within your Apple ID; the developer cannot read or access it. Without iCloud the game works identically — your progress is then kept on the device only. Apple's handling of iCloud data is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

In-App Purchases

Aransaga offers optional in-app purchases (story volumes) processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. The app itself never sees or stores any payment details. It only checks with Apple's StoreKit framework, on your device, whether a purchase exists, in order to unlock the corresponding content. Apple's handling of purchase data is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Apple's Game Center, Aransaga reports achievements you earn (reading progress, collectibles, side quests) to Game Center so they appear in your Game Center profile. This is optional: without a Game Center sign-in the game works identically and nothing is reported. Game Center is operated by Apple; the app never sees your Game Center friends or profile data beyond the sign-in status. Apple's handling of Game Center data is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Data We Do Not Collect

  • No account or sign-in required
  • No tracking — no advertising identifiers, no device ID, no cross-app or cross-site tracking of any kind
  • No location data
  • No contacts, camera, or microphone access
  • No crash reporting or analytics services are currently integrated
  • No data is shared with third parties for advertising or marketing

Children

Aransaga does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. The app contains no age-gated content or features requiring parental consent.
